Executive brief

What this tournament means for you

The 2025 edition drew 130 club scouts to Doha. Every group match is played across the pitches of one complex, so a single trip can cover several matches a day. The advantage goes to the teams who arrive knowing who to watch.

For clubs

  • One trip, 48 nations. We tell you which matches to attend and which players to follow in each.
  • Early sight of under-seen talent. Many African youth players are thinly covered by data platforms. Our full-match coverage puts them on your list before the market moves.
  • A pathway within the rules. FIFA's rules on players under 18 shape what is possible today. We map the route for each player, from academy and education pathways to the moves that open at 18.

For agents

  • The standouts in every African squad, identified as the tournament unfolds.
  • The role each player actually plays, read against peers from the same tournament.
  • Families and federations first. We work through them from day one, in line with FIFA's agent rules and its rules on minors.

For federations and academies

  • A benchmark against the world. See how your players compare with peers from Europe, South America and Asia.
  • Domestic and diaspora players tracked together, in one picture.
  • A talent report for each player after the tournament, ready for the next national-team cycle.
Our view

Morocco, Senegal, Mali, Cameroon and Côte d'Ivoire arrive with established youth pedigree. Tanzania's debut and Algeria's first appearance in 17 years add two squads few scouting departments have seen at this level. Those are the squads where an early look is worth most.

Players to watch

Our opening board, built from the U-17 Africa Cup of Nations and the squad announcements. It grows during the tournament as top scorers, standout goalkeepers, captains and decisive knockout performers from all ten squads emerge.

Ziyad Baha

Morocco · Striker

Playing his second U-17 World Cup in a row, a rare level of tournament experience at this age. Leads the line for the U-17 Africa Cup of Nations hosts, who topped their group.

MONITORFollow all three group matches, including Spain. A full scout card follows the group stage.

Ahmed Safwat

Egypt · Attacking midfielder · Captain · Zamalek

Egypt's captain and creator. Impressed at the U-17 Africa Cup of Nations with his passing and the chances he made against set defences.

MONITORFollow every Group A match, including the meeting with hosts Qatar. A full scout card follows the group stage.

Diego Elias Pelembe

Mozambique · Midfielder · Captain

Scored twice at the U-17 Africa Cup of Nations and leads a side that qualified through a play-off. Son of former Mozambique international Elias "Domingues" Pelembe.

MONITORFollow all three group matches. A full scout card follows the group stage.

Chris Karel

Cameroon · Captain

Captained Cameroon to the quarter-finals of the U-17 Africa Cup of Nations. The squad has been in Doha since 7 November, preparing on site.
